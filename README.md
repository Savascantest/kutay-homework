# French homework notebook

A privacy-safe, static French homework workspace. Each homework has its own tabs, and the archive keeps earlier material available without mixing lessons together. The current Unit 1 revision homework covers identity, contact-details vocabulary, numbers, places, and adjective agreement through 10 grammar questions, 10 vocabulary questions, 5 reading checks, an optional external three-exercise listening task, and a 10-question final quiz.

Future learner-facing exercises follow [the authoring standard](AUTHORING_GUIDE.md): natural contexts, one defensible answer, and concise explanations only after a correct answer.
