Grammar guide · identity, places & agreement

Build a clear profile one part at a time

Use the colours to see the job of each word: blue for the action, gold for the connector, green for the key information, and coral for a detail in the message.

1. Introduce yourself

Je m’appelle Lina.Je suis française.

Use je suis for a nationality or description. The adjective changes to match the person: français / française, turc / turque.

2. Say where someone lives

J’habite à Paris.Il habite en France.

Use à with a city, en with a feminine country, au with a masculine country, and aux with a plural country.

3. Say where someone is from

Elle vient de Paris.Il vient du Canada.

For origin, use de France, du Canada, and des États-Unis. The small word changes with the place.

4. Make the adjective agree

After être, a nationality agrees with the person.

  • Il est français.
  • Elle est française.
  • Ils sont français.
  • Elles sont françaises.

Read the whole profile aloud. Focus on the message, not the name of the rule.

Grammar guide · places, verbs & small words

Build the message in small, useful parts

French uses different small words for places. English often says in, but French changes the word depending on the place. Use the colours below to see the job of each part in a sentence.

1. Place words

Blue = action word. Gold = place link. Green = destination.

habite en Suisse
  • en Suisse — feminine country
  • au Canada — masculine country
  • aux États-Unis — plural country
  • à Lyon — city

2. Verb + place pattern

Use the same place word after several useful verbs.

J’habite à Lyon.Je vais au Canada.Elle arrive de France.

For origin, notice: de France, du Canada, des États-Unis, and de Paris.

3. Articles tell you about the noun

Green nouns need a small partner before them.

  • la musique — a feminine thing
  • le public — a masculine thing
  • un café — one masculine item
  • une bande dessinée — one feminine item
  • les billets — more than one item

Learn the article and noun together: say la gare, not only gare.

4. A message you can reuse

Coral shows the practical information in a message.

Bonjour ! Rendez-vous à la gare le vingt-et-un juillet.

Read the whole message aloud. Your goal is to communicate where, when, and with whom — not to memorise a grammar label.

Vocabulary guide · words in action

Learn a word, its partner, and a sentence

Click a flashcard to see French first and English second. Then use the guide cards to notice gender, common word partners, and the verbs that make the words useful in real messages.

1. Read the colour code

Blue = a verb or action. Gold = a linking word. Green = a person, place, or thing. Coral = practical message information.

Envoie un message ce soir.

2. Keep the article

French nouns have gender. Keep the small word with the noun whenever you revise.

  • un artiste / une artiste
  • une bande dessinée
  • le public
  • un rendez-vous
  • la gare

This makes it easier to build your own sentence later.

3. Learn useful chunks

Do not learn every word alone. Learn the words that naturally travel together.

avoir rendez-vous à la gareun billet pour le festivalle public écoute l’artiste

4. Verb patterns to reuse

Use aimer + infinitive when you like doing something:

J’aime dessiner des personnages.

Use envoyer + message for a quick instruction:

Envoie-moi un message demain.
