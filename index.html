Lesson notes

Useful French for people, places, and plans

Use à with cities, and choose the country word that fits the sentence. When you describe someone, pair where they are from with something they enjoy. In a message, dates and numbers need to be clear enough for another person to act on them.

Places

Profiles

Dates & numbers
